Statement on AI:

While I am passionate about creativity, another passion of mine is technology. AI or LLMs have arrived. Though many of us may yearn for a simpler time before these tools, I believe we need to adapt to a changing world and find a balance between human ingenuity and prompted constructions. There is an appropriate time and place for these tools. Creative expression can be one, but I do not consider that place to be the works of this site. These tools have been used to create this website, organizational help, and for research purposes. It is NEVER used to create the actual content.

-Ben
